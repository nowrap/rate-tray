# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## What is expected

- Keep discussion focused on the technical question at hand.
- Accept that maintainers and contributors have limited time and other priorities.
- When you disagree, argue the substance and be specific about what you would do differently.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, or level of experience.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where someone is
participating in this project.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening an issue, or privately via the contact address on the repository
owner's GitHub profile if the report should not be public.

Reports will be reviewed and acted on. Depending on severity that may mean a warning, editing
or removing the offending content, or blocking the account from the repository. Reporters'
identities will not be shared beyond what is necessary to act.

Maintainers who do not follow this document are subject to the same consequences as anyone
else.
